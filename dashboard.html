AS-recovery: does the agent rediscover analytical inventory-skew quoting?

How closely each agent's inventory-skew quoting matches the closed-form AS/GLFT curves (R²) — a sanity check on learned behavior, not a target to beat.

OOD transfer: agents evaluated outside their training regime

Each agent's checkpoint from one regime dropped into a different regime with no fine-tuning, across all 6 directed pairs among low_vol/normal/high_vol — tests generalization to market conditions never seen in training. QR-DQN stays profitable in all 6 of 6 transfers and actually improves on its in-distribution Sharpe in 3 of them; transferring into high_vol is the hardest direction for most agents.

Simulator validation — stylized facts

A random-policy audit of the simulator itself against 5 standard empirical market-microstructure regularities, independent of any trained agent — validates that the environment produces realistic dynamics to learn from.

CVaR efficient frontier — QR-DQN & IQN, normal regime

Mean P&L vs. tail risk (CVaR0.10) across the CVaR-α sweep, averaged over 4 training seeds per setting. Most α values are statistically indistinguishable once seed variance is accounted for — CVaR-α doesn't produce the smooth risk/return tradeoff the method is meant to deliver at this training budget.

Encoder ablation — QR-DQN, normal regime

QR-DQN's held-out Sharpe using handcrafted features vs. two learned snapshot representations (CNN, autoencoder) — handcrafted wins decisively (+4.19 vs. +0.45 and −0.18, both p<0.0001).

Encoder ablation — DQN, PPO, IQN, normal regime

The same CNN/autoencoder-vs-handcrafted comparison repeated for DQN, PPO, and IQN. Handcrafted significantly wins for DQN too, but shows no significant difference for PPO or IQN — because those two agents' own handcrafted baselines are themselves weak, not because their learned encoders work better.

Recurrent vs. snapshot: does temporal memory help?

Each agent's standard snapshot-encoder policy vs. the same agent with an LSTM backbone in its place, across 10 agent/regime/CVaR-risk combinations. Recurrent integration never significantly helps, significantly hurts QR-DQN and DQN in the normal regime, and its effect when combined with CVaR risk-sensitivity is alpha-dependent rather than a consistent interaction.
